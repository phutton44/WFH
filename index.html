API not reachable or server misconfigured

This app signs in against Vercel serverless routes (/api/*) and stores data in Neon Postgres. If you only opened static files (for example npx serve .), run vercel dev instead, or deploy to Vercel.

  1. In the Neon console, run neon/schema.sql on your database.
  2. In Vercel → your project → Settings → Environment Variables: set DATABASE_URL (from the Neon integration) and JWT_SECRET (any random string, at least 16 characters). Redeploy after saving.
  3. Confirm GET /api/health returns {"ok":true} on your deployment URL.

Day types

  • Office

    Working day you marked as physically in the office.

  • WFH

    Working day you marked as working from home (explicit WFH only).

  • Leave

    Annual leave on this date; counts toward your yearly allowance.

  • Sickness

    Paid or unpaid sick leave; does not use annual leave; excluded from office/WFH tracked totals like leave.

  • Unassigned

    Monday–Friday working day with no office, WFH, leave, sickness, or NWD yet.

  • NWD

    Non-working day you marked on the calendar; excluded from office/WFH and leave totals like a weekend.

  • Bank hol.

    England & Wales public holiday on a weekday (non-working).

  • Weekend

    Saturday or Sunday (always non-working).
